Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Who we are

Full Plate Intelligence provides insights, benchmarking, and practical strategies for independent restaurant owners, including our email newsletter, Full Plate Weekly.

What we collect

When you sign up for Full Plate Weekly, we collect the email address you provide. We also record which signup form on our website you used (for example, the homepage or the Meet Steve page) so we can understand how people find us. Our website does not ask for any other personal information at signup.

Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically log basic technical information such as IP addresses and browser types for security and operational purposes.

How we use your information

We use your email address to send you Full Plate Weekly and other Full Plate Intelligence communications, such as updates about our upcoming shows, reports, and tools. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link included in every email, or by contacting us directly.

Email service provider

We use Kit (kit.com), an email service provider, to manage our mailing list and send our emails. When you subscribe, your email address is stored and processed by Kit on our behalf, subject to Kit's own privacy policy and terms.

Sharing

We do not sell your email address. We share it only with service providers (such as Kit) that help us operate our mailing list and website.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in any of our emails. If you would like your email address removed from our list entirely, contact us at Steve@FullPlateIntel.com and we will take care of it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as Full Plate Intelligence grows. The latest version will always be posted on this page.

Contact
