Why I Started
Full Plate Intelligence

Over the years I noticed a pattern.
The same questions kept coming up.

The answers existed.
They just weren’t accessible.

Large restaurant groups have teams of analysts, CFOs, benchmarking data, and sophisticated reporting. Independent operators rarely do.

Full Plate Intelligence
was created to change that.

My goal is to give independent restaurant owners access to practical financial intelligence: through weekly insights, benchmarking, conversations with industry leaders, and tools that help them make better decisions with confidence.
